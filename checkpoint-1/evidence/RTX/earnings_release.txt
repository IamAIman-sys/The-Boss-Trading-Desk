RTX Reports Q3 2026 Results
Strong operational performance drives double-digit sales and earnings growth in Q3;
Raises 2026 outlook for adjusted sales,* adjusted EPS,* and free cash flow*
ARLINGTON, Va., October 20, 2026 – RTX (NYSE: RTX) reports third quarter 2026 results.
Third quarter 2026
•Sales of $25.0 billion, up 11 percent versus prior year, and up 12 percent organically*
•GAAP EPS of $1.89, including $0.26 of acquisition accounting adjustments and $0.11 of restructuring and other net significant and/or non-recurring items
•Adjusted EPS* of $2.26, up $0.56, or 33 percent, versus prior year, including $0.25 from IEEPA cost recovery
•Operating cash flow of $2.9 billion, including approximately $0.3 billion from IEEPA recovery; free cash flow* of $2.1 billion
•Company backlog of $340 billion, including $176 billion of commercial and $164 billion of defense
•Awarded Tomahawk and AMRAAM multi-year undefinitized production contracts in the third quarter
Updates outlook for full year 2026
•Adjusted sales* of $97.0 - $97.5 billion, up from $95.0 - $96.0 billion
•Organic sales growth* of 10 to 11 percent, up from 8 to 9 percent
•Adjusted EPS* of $7.65 - $7.75, up from $7.10 - $7.25
•Free cash flow* of $9.0 - $9.2 billion, up from $8.50 - $8.75 billion
“Our strong execution and operational focus in the third quarter enabled RTX to deliver double-digit organic sales growth* across commercial aerospace and defense. Demand for our products remains robust with over $81 billion of new commercial and defense awards, resulting in a record backlog of $340 billion,” said RTX Chairman and CEO Chris Calio.
“Given our performance year to date, we are again raising our full year outlook for adjusted sales,* adjusted EPS,* and free cash flow.* We remain focused on increasing output, optimizing our cost structure, and investing in new technologies to support our customers and drive continued growth.”

Third quarter 2026
RTX third quarter reported and adjusted sales* were $25.0 billion, up 11 percent versus the prior year and 12 percent organically.* GAAP EPS of $1.89 included $0.26 of acquisition accounting adjustments and $0.11 of restructuring and other net significant and/or non-recurring items. Adjusted EPS* of $2.26 was up 33 percent versus the prior year, including $0.25 of IEEPA recovery.
The company reported net income attributable to common shareowners in the third quarter of $2.6 billion, which included $0.3 billion of acquisition accounting adjustments and $0.2 billion of restructuring and other net significant and/or non-recurring items. Adjusted net income* of $3.1 billion was up 34 percent versus the prior year driven by $1.1 billion of adjusted segment operating profit growth* across all three segments, including $0.4 billion of IEEPA recovery. This growth was partially offset by higher taxes and lower pension income. Operating cash flow in the third quarter was $2.9 billion, including $0.3 billion of IEEPA recovery. Capital expenditures were $0.7 billion, resulting in free cash flow* of $2.1 billion.

Collins Aerospace third quarter 2026 reported and adjusted sales* of $8,382 million were up 10 percent versus the prior year. Excluding the impact of divestitures, sales increased 12 percent organically* driven by a 23 percent increase in commercial OE, a 9 percent increase in commercial aftermarket, and a 7 percent increase in defense. The increase in commercial OE sales was driven by higher volume on narrowbody and widebody platforms, while the increase in commercial aftermarket sales was driven by growth in modifications and upgrades, provisioning, and parts and repair. The increase in defense sales was driven by higher volume across multiple programs.
Collins Aerospace reported operating profit of $1,789 million was up 42 percent versus the prior year. Reported operating profit in Q3 2025 included a gain on the sale of the actuation and flight control business. Adjusted operating profit* of $1,788 million was up 50 percent versus the prior year. The increase was driven by drop through on higher commercial volume, commercial mix, and approximately $250 million of IEEPA recovery.

Pratt & Whitney third quarter reported and adjusted sales* of $9,231 million were up 10 percent versus the prior year. The sales growth was driven by a 21 percent increase in commercial aftermarket and a 2 percent increase in military, partially offset by a 5 percent decrease in commercial OE. Use and Definitions of Non-GAAP Financial Measures
RTX Corporation (“RTX” or “the Company”) reports its financial results in accordance with accounting principles generally accepted in the United States (“GAAP”). We supplement the reporting of our financial information determined under GAAP with certain non-GAAP financial information. The non-GAAP information presented provides investors with additional useful information but should not be considered in isolation or as substitutes for the related GAAP measures. We believe that these non-GAAP measures provide investors with additional insight into the Company’s ongoing business performance. Other companies may define non-GAAP measures differently, which limits the usefulness of these measures for comparisons with such other companies. We encourage investors to review our financial statements and publicly-filed reports in their entirety and not to rely on any single financial measure.
